An agent that spends your money under a limit you signed — and every payment carries proof it never went over.

SERV Reasoning decides what to buy and when to stop. A Kite Passport session, approved with a passkey and held outside this program, decides whether the purchase is allowed. Nothing in this repository can raise that limit.

Judge it in 60 seconds

  1. The run above shows a paid purchase, then a purchase the session refused, then another it refused for a different reason.
  2. Open /proof.json. Every payment there is re-read from the Kite chain at request time and compared against the receipt, so the page reports what the chain says, not what this repository claims.
  3. Each paid step carries a transaction hash. Open it on KiteScan and you will see the USDC transfer to the service's address.

The refusal is the point. An agent that limits itself is keeping a promise. An agent whose limit is a signed session on someone else's chain is obeying something it cannot edit.
